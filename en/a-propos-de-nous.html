About the firm

Maître Chakouri Belkacem specialises in business law, banking law, insurance law, commercial law and contract law, and more generally assists his clients in matters of contract drafting, joint ventures between companies, real estate law, criminal law and family law, as well as employment law and criminal defence, and is active in the fields of competition law and business litigation.

He completed outstanding studies (top of his class) in legal science and political science in France and Canada, and is fully trilingual.

He took over the firm in 1994 (Toque 1594) and developed its practice locally, by acting throughout Morocco and defending the interests of foreign groups, and internationally, by entering into a partnership (Co-operation and Alliance Agreement) with the “Shanghai Landing Law Firm”, which has more than 700 associates, and by acting abroad in matters relating to major civil engineering projects (PPP/BOT), the conclusion of partnership agreements between companies, and in the healthcare sector.

The Chakouri firm has built an international network of lawyers in America, Canada, Europe, the Maghreb and sub-Saharan Africa, as well as in India and China.

The firm has received international recognition on several occasions and is listed for the quality of its practice. The firm is also a member of the A.E.A.

CHAKOURI LAWYERS FIRM has received several international awards, having been named among the best lawyers in Morocco for the quality of its practice in business law, litigation and arbitration in 2017, 2018 and 2019, and in international litigation and arbitration in 2020.

In conclusion, Maître Belkacem CHAKOURI attaches the utmost importance to human relationships. In the exercise of his duties, this is reflected in the rigour and effort he puts into winning his cases, and in his humility and simplicity, which mean that clients easily become attached to him, as he explains the law and makes it accessible to his clients, with whom he is in constant dialogue in order to serve them as well as possible.

Our vision

The firm is a legal player with a strong reputation in its sector in Morocco. Its lawyers provide its clients with quality legal services and cutting-edge advisory work. The firm offers strategic advice and practical solutions to enable its diverse clientele to achieve the proper settlement of their disputes and to act with full knowledge of the facts in their own environment when handling legal matters. The firm becomes a trusted partner for its clients by providing them with quality legal assistance, an integrated and diversified offering, up-to-date legal monitoring, and a charter of values that it upholds through the GESICA international network of lawyers, which it represents in Morocco.
