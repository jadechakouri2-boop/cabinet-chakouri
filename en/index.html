Lawyer Maître Chakouri

Maître Chakouri Belkacem specialises in business law, banking law, insurance law, commercial law and contract law, and more generally assists his clients in matters of contract drafting, joint ventures between companies, real estate law, criminal law and family law, as well as employment law and white-collar criminal defence, and is active in the fields of competition law and litigation.

CHAKOURI LAWYERS FIRM is a member of the GESICA international network of lawyers, which it represents in Morocco.

02

Mastered business law serving your interests

Mr. El Mostafa Ammas

Lawyer – Doctor of Laws | University Professor | Business Law Expert

Mr. El Mostafa Ammas joined the Firm in 2016. With nearly ten years of experience in legal practice, he brings clients a legal expertise combining academic excellence, intellectual rigor and a thorough command of the economic and strategic challenges facing businesses.

Holder of a Doctorate in Law, a university professor and researcher, he draws on an outstanding academic background complemented by training in business management and accounting. This dual expertise gives him a cross-disciplinary approach, enabling him to deliver legal solutions tailored to the complex issues faced by economic operators.

His practice focuses primarily on business law, with recognized expertise in dispute resolution, competition law, intellectual property, employment law, civil law, real estate and construction law, corporate law, governance and compliance (Corporate & Compliance), commercial law, large-scale retail, as well as customs and maritime law.

Convinced that the value of legal advice lies as much in its technical rigor as in its strategic vision, Mr. El Mostafa Ammas supports his clients in risk prevention, securing their operations and defending their interests before the courts and competent authorities. His approach is distinguished by thorough case analysis, constant availability and an absolute commitment to quality of service.

Alongside his work as a lawyer, he pursues an academic career as a professor and researcher, actively contributing to the development of legal doctrine and to training the next generation of jurists and business leaders. This complementarity between professional practice and teaching strengthens his expertise and allows him to offer clients guidance grounded in the most recent developments in the law.

Excellence, rigor, independence and commitment are the principles that guide his daily practice of the profession, in the service of individuals, businesses and investors.

03

Our history

  1. 1951

    The Chakouri firm was founded in 1951 by the late Maître Mohamed CHAKOURI, who was one of the first lawyers in Morocco (Toque 7) and who always enjoyed great renown, both as a lawyer and as a man.

  2. 1994

    His son, Maître Belkacem CHAKOURI, joined his firm in 1994 after completing outstanding studies (top of his class) in law and political science in France and Canada.

  3. 2026

    75 years of experience and expertise.

Maître CHAKOURI specialises in business law, banking law, insurance law, contract law, family law, digital law and real estate law. However, CHAKOURI LAWYERS FIRM, which has seven associates, generally handles all kinds of matters concerning companies and individuals, and also provides a Compliance service for the whole of Africa in collaboration with the only data company.

We aim to stay close to our clients, and to that end we have deliberately chosen to remain a human-scale firm that is accessible to all.

Our firm is recognised both locally and internationally and has received several awards.

We have been ranked by the international magazine DÉCIDEURS as one of the best law firms in Morocco, in the “Highly recommended” category for business law and the “Recommended” category for arbitration & litigation.

We are committed to preserving our reputation for reliability, honesty and efficiency, handed down by the founder.

The firm has a well-developed pro bono practice, providing its assistance free of charge in defending people in need, as well as to non-profit organisations, single mothers and battered women.

Our practice areas

As a lawyer, my objective is to provide quality legal services and to represent my clients with integrity and dedication. We offer a wide range of legal services in various areas of law. My personalised approach to cases allows me to understand the unique needs of each client and to work with them to find effective solutions. I am convinced that my skills, experience and passion for the law enable me to provide excellent service to my clients.

  • Criminal law

    The Chakouri firm has lawyers specialising in criminal law and white-collar crime. They are fully committed to representing their clients before the criminal courts, defending their cases with vigour and energy.

  • Contract law

    When drafting a document, it is important to set out the requirements precisely and to anticipate possible conflict situations in order to avoid disputes. The firm’s role is to ensure rigorous drafting that reassures its clients.

  • Real estate law

    Our real estate law firm in Morocco offers its clients an attentive ear and expert advice to help them navigate the complexity of land types and payment methods. We ensure that transactions proceed without surprises.

  • Business law

    Our business law firm places itself at the service of its clients to provide them with practical and reliable solutions. We offer informed advice in all situations so that the client feels taken care of.

  • Banking law

    Our firm specialises in banking law and offers valuable expertise in banking litigation proceedings, particularly in debt recovery for payment incidents, unpaid sums, unrepaid loans and other account management problems. We have a sound knowledge of the regulatory law in this field.
