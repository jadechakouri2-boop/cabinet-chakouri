TRANSPARENCY
CHARTER

Our principles are intended to help everyone at the firm act in compliance with the applicable laws, rules and regulations, but also, and beyond that, with the firm’s policies and standards, which it restates.

  • We are a law firm. We have a personal and professional obligation to know the law, to apply it and to respect it.

  • As a law firm, we have an obligation to understand and comply with the ethical standards set by the courts, the bar and the professional associations to which we belong.

  • We treat others with honesty and dignity. There is no place for prejudice, discrimination or abusive behaviour in our firm.

  • We maintain honest relationships with our clients, our competitors and one another. We do not engage in any act of corruption and do not allow anyone to do so on our behalf.

  • We protect the information entrusted to us. Sensitive and confidential information is entrusted to us. We must preserve its confidentiality.

  • We are bound by our words. We are honest in our dealings with our clients, with one another and with everyone we work with. We record our work and our time accurately and truthfully. We invoice in accordance with the applicable agreements.

  • We act responsibly.

  • As a responsible corporate citizen, we are committed to using resources respectfully.

Diversity and inclusion

We strive to cultivate a respectful and enriching environment that encourages diverse individuals to flourish personally and professionally as contributors to the firm’s success. We neither permit nor tolerate discrimination in the workplace.

Uncompromising on quality

Our reputation rests on the quality of our lawyers and legal professionals, our advice and our services. Entertainment and corporate gifts Our principles encourage us to get to know our clients personally; however, we must bear in mind that client relationships remain professional relationships and that certain limits must not be crossed.

We must therefore be particularly vigilant and must not offer, give, solicit or accept gifts, entertainment or other similar benefits for the purpose of obtaining or retaining a matter or of unduly influencing the client’s decisions or business activities, or our own conduct.

Prevention of money laundering

We do not take part in money laundering activities, nor do we help clients to take part in them, and we act in accordance with the law, in particular Law 43-05 on combating money laundering and the persons subject to it.

Let us avoid conflicts of interest

We must avoid any investment, interest or association that interferes with the independent exercise of our personal judgement and our professional obligations, in the best interests of the firm and its clients.

Let us comply with anti-corruption laws We must comply with anti-corruption laws, which apply in particular to public authorities and public officials and
which also cover our relationships with clients and suppliers, as well as with third parties acting on our behalf.

Protection of confidential information

As lawyers, we have access to sensitive confidential information. The trust our clients place in us is founded on their knowing that we can and
will preserve the confidentiality of the information they share with us.

It is incumbent upon us to protect and preserve the confidentiality of client and firm information, except where its disclosure is expressly requested by the firm under the rules of professional responsibility to which we are subject, or as required by law. This entails, in particular, full respect for personal data.

Use of the firm’s information systems

Our information systems must be used in compliance with legal and regulatory rules, including the requirements of the Bar Association, in particular when they are used by a member of the firm for purposes other than professional practice.

Use of social media

The firm is subject to obligations to protect privacy, confidentiality and the interests of the firm and its clients, and undertakes not to use these media inappropriately.

Accordingly, no communication may take place on social media on behalf of the firm without the firm’s prior express consent.

Environmental protection

We are aware of the importance of sustainable development for the members of the firm, our clients and the communities in which we live and work.

Community service and pro bono activities

We strive to use our legal knowledge to serve the rights of people in need.

Pro bono activities are subject to compliance with all our professional and ethical obligations, including, in particular, those relating to conflicts of interest.

FEE
TRANSPARENCY
CHARTER

The GESICA network brings together independent law firms that have chosen to emphasise quality of communication and fee transparency between its members and their clients.

GESICA lawyers, while respecting the freedom to set fees but concerned with providing precise prior information, undertake, beyond their legal obligations, to ensure complete and honest clarity on the costs of their services by observing the following rules:

  1. Provide prior information on the amount of advance payments and fees (fixed fee) or on the method of determining it (time spent), or propose a fee agreement that may include a success fee.

  2. Set out in writing a proposal for fees, costs and disbursements, to be submitted for the client’s signature or approval.

  3. Notify the client of any event likely to change the amount initially envisaged as a result of developments in the case.

  4. Provide, on simple request, a detailed statement of the work carried out.

  5. For time-based fees, invoice progressively as the work is carried out and keep the client regularly informed.

  6. Give the client, at the end of the assignment entrusted to the lawyer or on closure of the file, a detailed account specifying the work performed and listing all sums called for.

  7. Display this charter and bring it to the attention of clients.

In accordance with the provisions of Article 10 of the RIN, this charter may be brought to the attention of the public.
