Banking law

Banking law has developed rapidly in Morocco in recent years, becoming increasingly subject to public regulation. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Intellectual property law is the law that protects intangible rights. Intellectual property law mainly includes: copyright, trade mark law, design law and patent law. Also included are: rights related to copyright, the sui generis database right, semiconductor topographies, plant varieties and utility certificates. The best intellectual property is the one that is taken care of as early as possible and properly understood, since initial mistakes can have unfortunate consequences many years later. Given the spread of trade mark imitations, approximations intended to mislead people, illegal intellectual appropriation and fraudulent operations involving patents, our firm’s assistance will be very useful to you, whether by anticipating certain actions or by defending you if you are the victim of abuse.

CIVIL LAW

Civil law governs relations between persons in all areas of everyday life involving rights and obligations. Accordingly, everyone should know this branch of the law or be assisted by a qualified lawyer when a problem arises between persons bound by agreements, or before a problem arises, by taking preventive action, that is to say by seeking advice from a lawyer. At the Chakouri firm, you will find qualified legal professionals ready to listen to you, assist you and advise you, meeting your expectations and finding solutions to the problems you may face.

DIVORCE AND FAMILY LAWYERS

DIVORCE AND FAMILY LAWYERS A divorce is never a mere formality. Even an amicable divorce can be complicated. The financial aspects are also extremely important, and you must secure your future so that you can provide for your needs decently. While a divorce is only a decision between two adults, the decisions that are made will have consequences for an entire family and can sometimes even affect work, friends and living environment. For all these reasons, divorce is a judicial procedure that must be conducted by an experienced lawyer. You need to be assisted and represented by a lawyer who is well versed in divorce proceedings, a lawyer who will be able to help you get through this divorce as simply and quickly as possible: a single oversight could turn your divorce into a bitter and devastating battle. You must not take any risks! Drawing on our experience, we are able to assist you during your divorce or family dispute. The firm also specialises in divorces involving foreign nationals and dual nationals.

DIGITAL AND INTERNET LAW

Digital law is the branch of law specific to new technologies. It governs the problems created by the emergence of the information society and is mainly concerned with the protection of privacy, which is undermined by the computerised collection of data. We can no longer speak of a virtual law but of a real law, since it profoundly affects our everyday lives and those of our children. We can no longer speak of virtuality, but of reality. The GAFAs have created technological tools without giving any thought to after-sales service, so that they themselves are now overwhelmed by the extent to which events, and above all news stories, can no longer be controlled. The applications we know and this profusion of social networks lead us, despite ourselves, to disclose our personal information without being aware of it, and any information given or published, any photo, any opinion, may resurface when we least expect it and turn our lives upside down. Do not hesitate to call on our firm for any problem you may encounter in this field.

INSURANCE LAW

Our firm handles insurance litigation, combining efficiency and speed. The field of insurance is essential, as it governs and encompasses all activities concerning natural and legal persons, transport, all types of natural or supernatural events and, in short, all the acts that characterise social life. The insurance contract is a legal protection based on three elements: a risk (a dispute), a premium, and the provision of benefits should the risk materialise. In the event of a dispute, both amicable and judicial claims may be considered. Our firm is well versed in insurance law, particularly in the speedy handling of insurance case proceedings, and can provide advice on all matters in this field.

Fees:
HOW MUCH DOES A LAWYER COST?

A lawyer’s fees are freely set by agreement with the client. There is no indicative scale. It is therefore necessary to discuss with your lawyer the arrangements for determining the amount of the fees.

The fixed fee

From the outset of their relationship, the lawyer and the client agree on an overall fee for the services defined between them. Beyond that, any service not provided for will be subject to additional remuneration.

The time-based fee

The lawyer and the client agree on an hourly rate from the outset. The final fee will be calculated by the lawyer at the end of the case.

The success fee

It is not possible to determine a lawyer’s fees on the basis of winning the case or according to the result obtained.

However, it is possible to agree on a basic fee (fixed or hourly), supplemented by additional remuneration calculated according to the result obtained. This additional fee must be the subject of a prior agreement.

Good to know:

Whatever arrangement is chosen, it is advisable to set it down in writing with your lawyer. Your lawyer’s fees are not necessarily the only expense incurred in legal proceedings. Known as expenses or costs, these additional outlays generally cover procedural costs or the remuneration of other officers of the court (for example, the services of a bailiff). Any dispute concerning fees falls within the jurisdiction of the President of the Bar (Bâtonnier), to whom you may write by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt, setting out the reasons why you are contesting your lawyer’s fees.
